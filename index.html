How booking works

Your form is a request. We confirm availability, price and the final booking with you.

  1. Send your request

    Fill the short form with your service, property, location and preferred date.

  2. We review and quote

    Your request goes straight to our team. A Kadibis representative contacts you, inspects the property if needed and sends a quotation.

  3. Approve and we schedule

    Once you approve the quote, we confirm your booking, assign a team and get the job done properly.

Questions before you book

Is my request a confirmed booking?

Not yet. A Kadibis representative reviews it and contacts you to discuss the job, inspection if needed, the quotation and the final booking.

Do I pay when I send a request?

No. Sending a request is free. Price and any deposit are discussed with you before the booking is confirmed.

Which cities do you serve?

Port Harcourt, Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan and Kwara. If you are somewhere else, choose Other and tell us your location.

Can I get painting and cleaning together?

Yes. Choose Both in step one and the same team can paint, then clean up so the space is ready to use.

Can I change my preferred date?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we will move you to the next available slot.

Do you work on large sites?

We handle homes, shops, offices and industrial spaces. Tell us the size in the notes and we will plan the right team.
